# Research routing

Choose one primary route; add others only when the question crosses fields.

| Route | Use for | Method |
|---|---|---|
| Business and markets | Ideas, demand, competitors, industries | `methods/business.md` |
| Technology and products | Software, services, devices | `methods/technology-products.md` |
| Science and health | Scientific and clinical evidence | `methods/science-health.md` |
| Economics and finance | Indicators, companies, financial scenarios | `methods/economics-finance.md` |
| Society, policy and education | Demographics, public opinion, programs | `methods/society-policy-education.md` |
| Law and regulation | Rules, cases, jurisdictions | `methods/law-regulation.md` |
| Engineering | Systems, materials, standards, safety | `methods/engineering.md` |
| Data analysis | User datasets and statistical analysis | `methods/data-analysis.md` |

## General route
For topics outside the listed routes, use the general workflow in `research-core.md`.

## Cross-disciplinary work
Load only relevant methods. Keep definitions and assumptions consistent. Explain incompatible measures rather than combining them.

## Depth
- **Concise:** narrow, stable question; few strong sources.
- **Standard:** several questions or alternatives; triangulate evidence.
- **In-depth:** complex, high-stakes, or contested topic; examine methods, counter-evidence, and uncertainty.

Choose depth automatically. Do not ask the user unless their preference materially changes the work.
