# Economics and finance research

Use for macroeconomics, industries, company finances, financial instruments, and economic scenarios.

Define geography, period, population, currency, and indicator definitions. Prefer official statistical agencies, central banks, regulators, audited filings, and transparent datasets. Check revisions, seasonal adjustment, base years, and data coverage.

Distinguish nominal and real values, stocks and flows, annual and quarterly rates, and correlation and causation. Explain formulas, units, assumptions, and uncertainty. For forecasts, use explicit scenarios and sensitivity analysis; do not present projections as facts or guarantees.

For company analysis, distinguish reported results from adjusted measures and analyst estimates. For financial products, disclose fees, liquidity, risk, time horizon, and data date. Do not give individualized investment recommendations.
