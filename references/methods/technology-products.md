# Technology and product research

Use for software, digital services, hardware, platforms, and technical products.

Compare purpose, architecture where relevant, functions, performance, compatibility, security, privacy, accessibility, reliability, support, update history, licensing, total cost of ownership, and limitations.

Prefer official documentation, release notes, standards, pricing pages, and independent tests. Distinguish vendor claims from independently measured results. Record product version, test conditions, date, and configuration. Do not compare benchmark results obtained under materially different conditions without explaining the difference.

For software, consider deployment, integrations, data portability, lock-in, maintenance, and operational requirements. For hardware, consider specifications, test methods, safety certifications, warranty, repairability, and lifecycle costs.

Conclude with suitability by use case, trade-offs, unknowns, and checks the buyer should perform.
