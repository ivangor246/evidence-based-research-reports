# Engineering and technical research

Use for engineering systems, materials, equipment, processes, performance, and safety.

Define operating conditions, loads, environment, units, tolerances, lifecycle, and applicable jurisdiction or standards. Prefer current standards, technical specifications, peer-reviewed engineering work, official safety notices, and independent test data.

Record test setup, assumptions, uncertainty, margins, failure modes, maintenance, and safety constraints. Check dimensional consistency and calculation methods. Distinguish nominal specifications from measured performance. Do not certify designs or claim compliance without evidence and qualified verification.
