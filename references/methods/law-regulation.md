# Law and regulation research

Use for legislation, regulatory requirements, public rules, and case-law research.

Establish jurisdiction, relevant dates, sector, parties, and the exact legal question. Prefer official legislation databases, regulators, courts, and government guidance. Check whether provisions are in force, amended, repealed, proposed, or subject to transitional rules.

Separate the text of the law, official interpretation, court decisions, and analytical commentary. Cite specific provisions and dates. Explain conflicts and hierarchy of authority. Do not treat rules from one jurisdiction as universal or provide a binding legal opinion. Flag questions requiring qualified local counsel.
