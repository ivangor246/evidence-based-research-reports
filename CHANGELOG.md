# Changelog

## 1.0.0
- Initial public release of Evidence-Based Research & Reports.
- Includes eight specialist research methods and a general research workflow.
- Responds and writes reports in the language of the user's request.
- Includes source evaluation, claim tracking, and localized PDF labels.
- Dual-licensed under Apache-2.0 OR MIT.
- Removed visible evidence-type tags from PDF text while retaining internal claim metadata.
- Strengthened claim-to-source validation and flagged unused bibliography entries.
- Kept executive summaries concise while allowing length and format to fit the research.
- Reworked business idea scoring to separate problem evidence from commercial validation.
- Added idea diversification, competitor/substitute analysis, and multi-channel acquisition research.
- Added compact source entries, clickable titles, optional table column widths, and automatic contents for long reports.
- Improved PDF typography and table readability.
- Made claim classification optional for ordinary prose and retained source checks for cited claims.
- Made business scoring and validation methods adaptable to the research question.
- Restricted data-leakage checks to predictive modelling.
- Added multilingual font fallback, CJK support, and explicit RTL shaping dependencies.
- Made contents generation depend on document length and hardened malformed-JSON validation.
