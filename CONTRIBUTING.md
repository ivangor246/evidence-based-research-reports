# Contributing

Suggestions, bug reports, and improvements are welcome.

- Open an issue to describe a problem or propose a change.
- Keep changes focused and instructions concise.
- Explain why a change improves research quality, reliability, or usability.
- Run `pytest` before submitting a pull request.
- Do not include private data, credentials, or copyrighted material without permission.
- Keep the skill's language-neutral behavior and existing license terms.

By submitting a contribution, you agree that it may be distributed under the project's dual license: MIT or Apache 2.0.
